SALES DATA ANALYSIS REPORT
==========================

1. Objective
-------------
The objective of this task is to analyze sales data using Python and Pandas.
The analysis focuses on total sales, average order value, top-performing
products, category-wise sales, region-wise sales, and monthly sales.

2. Total Sales
--------------
Total Sales: ₹557,800

3. Average Order Value
----------------------
Average Order Value: ₹27,890

4. Top Products
---------------
Laptop generated the highest sales among all products.
Monitor and Printer were the next highest-performing products.

5. Category-wise Analysis
-------------------------
The Electronics category generated higher sales compared with the
Accessories category.

6. Region-wise Analysis
-----------------------
Sales were analyzed across North, South, East, and West regions to
identify regional sales performance.

7. Time-period Analysis
-----------------------
Sales were grouped by month to understand sales performance over the
available time period.

8. Tools Used
-------------
Python
Pandas
Excel

9. Conclusion
-------------
The analysis demonstrates how Pandas can be used to calculate important
sales metrics and summarize business data. The results help identify
high-performing products, categories, and regions and provide useful
insights for business decision-making.
